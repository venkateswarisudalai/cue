Installing Vantage

1. Drag Vantage into the Applications folder.
2. Open Vantage from Applications.

The first time, macOS may say it can't verify the developer (Vantage isn't
notarized yet). Click Done, then open System Settings > Privacy & Security,
scroll down, and click "Open Anyway" next to Vantage.

Needs an Apple silicon Mac on macOS 26 (Tahoe) or later. For AI notes, open
Vantage > Settings > AI and pick one: a free local model (Ollama, LM Studio),
your own API key (OpenRouter, Groq, Gemini, OpenAI, Mistral, DeepSeek,
Together), any OpenAI-compatible server, or Claude.
